# Roadmap

This roadmap records demand-driven possibilities, not delivery commitments.

## Beta 1 maintenance

- Track ARD and hf-discover wire changes through pinned fixtures.
- Stabilize provider ergonomics from independent integration feedback.
- Improve diagnostics without broadening eligibility semantics.
- Archive the release and record a concept and version DOI when Zenodo is configured.

## Candidate follow-up work

- ARD observed-fitness evidence providers.
- Region and residency policy facts.
- Authentication and dependency feasibility providers.
- Install-permission envelopes without automatic installation.
- Additional registries and clients with active maintainer demand.
- Public product-only benchmarks over open fixtures.

## Explicitly out of scope

- A universal trust score.
- Replacing relevance ranking.
- Automatic resource invocation or remediation.
- Building or operating a registry.
- Adding ecosystem integrations without a reproduced technical seam.
