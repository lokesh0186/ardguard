The product story

Discovery finds. ARDGuard decides eligibility. Your runtime invokes.

01

Discover

A backend returns ranked candidates and relevance scores.

02

Establish facts

Configured providers establish capability, evidence, access, and authority facts.

03

Apply requirements

The task states what must be true for a candidate to be eligible.

04

Select

ARDGuard chooses the highest-ranked eligible candidate or fails closed.

05

Invoke elsewhere

Your runtime keeps final authorization and invocation ownership.
